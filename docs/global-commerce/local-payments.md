# Local Payments

SolydFlow enables businesses to accept local payments by integrating with supported payment providers.

Payment methods are provided by the configured payment provider rather than by SolydFlow itself.

## Supported Providers

Depending on your integration, SolydFlow can route transactions through providers such as:

* Paystack
* Flutterwave
* Monnify
* Stripe
* M-Pesa

Available payment methods depend on the selected provider and the countries it supports.

## M-Pesa

Unlike other payment methods, SolydFlow provides direct support for M-Pesa integration for supported regions.
