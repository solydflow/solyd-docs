# International Payments

SolydFlow allows businesses to sell across multiple countries using localized Products and supported payment providers.

## Payment Processing

Payments are processed by the configured payment provider.

SolydFlow does not receive, hold, or settle customer funds.

Instead, SolydFlow manages:

* Purchase orchestration
* Transaction tracking
* Recovery
* Reconciliation
* Entitlement delivery

Settlement remains the responsibility of your payment provider.

## Cross-Border Commerce

Businesses can configure Products with multiple currency Packages, allowing customers in different regions to purchase the same offering using localized pricing.
