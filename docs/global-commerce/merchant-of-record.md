# Merchant of Record

> **Status:** Under Development

A Merchant of Record (MoR) acts as the legal seller of a product or service, handling responsibilities such as payment processing, tax collection, invoicing, and regulatory compliance on behalf of a business.

Merchant of Record services simplify global commerce by allowing businesses to sell internationally without managing many of the operational and compliance requirements themselves.

## Planned Integrations

SolydFlow is currently exploring integrations with Merchant of Record providers including:

* Dodo Payments
* Paddle
* Lemon Squeezy

Additional providers may be supported in the future.

If there is a Merchant of Record service you would like SolydFlow to support, please let us know through our support channels.
