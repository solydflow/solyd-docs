# Tax & Compliance

> **Status:** Under Development

SolydFlow plans to provide tax calculation capabilities to improve transaction visibility for businesses operating across multiple regions.

## Tax Calculations

Future tax support will calculate applicable taxes and present them alongside transaction data.

Tax calculations are intended to provide transparency and reporting insights.

## Merchant of Record

Tax collection and remittance remain the responsibility of your configured Merchant of Record or payment provider.

SolydFlow does not currently collect or remit taxes on behalf of businesses.
